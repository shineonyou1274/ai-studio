### Part 5. Hybrid Production Workflow (Code + AI Integration)
To save time and preserve precise rhythm sync, follow this hybrid assembly method:
1. **Base Layer:** Use your code-based motion graphics (tunnel grids, caution tape vectors, musical staves) as the rhythmic backbone.
2. **Character Layer:** Overlay the AI-generated 2.5D virtual idol clips (`RIA` and `ZET`) on top using screen/overlay blending modes or picture-in-picture frames.
3. **UI Polish:** Ensure lyrics are placed in the lower 30% area with a subtle 50% black shadow box behind the text so they remain 100% readable against the glowing neon yellow elements.
